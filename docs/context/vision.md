# UNGANE – Vision

## Mission

Help African businesses build stronger customer relationships through messaging — attract, understand, engage, convert, retain, and grow.

## Core Idea

Businesses already communicate with customers on messaging platforms (WhatsApp first; Facebook Messenger next). UNGANE is the intelligence and relationship layer on top of those conversations: CRM, engagement, automation, and (eventually) AI assistance — without requiring customers to download a UNGANE app.

## Positioning

We are **not** selling “WhatsApp automation” alone.

We are selling: an AI-powered customer relationship and engagement platform that helps African businesses grow through conversations.

Primary path:

**Conversation → Customer → Relationship → Engagement → Retention → Growth**

Commerce (quotes, orders, payments) may extend this later; relationship management remains the center. UNGANE is not a conversational-commerce / payments / logistics stack first.

## Why It Exists

Businesses in Congo (and across Africa) already use WhatsApp daily, but manually:

- Customer numbers saved randomly
- No history or segmentation
- Missed follow-ups and promotions
- No structured feedback or reactivation

UNGANE turns those conversations into a complete customer relationship system without requiring technical knowledge.

## Target Market

- Primary: Democratic Republic of Congo (Congo-first)
- Language: French-first; eventual African languages (e.g. Lingala, Swahili)
- Users: small businesses — salons, spas, restaurants, shops, clinics, gyms, barbers, consultants, mechanics
- Behavior: mobile-first, often on low-end devices, WhatsApp-heavy habits
- Localization is product design, not UI translation only

## What We Are Not

Not initially: full ERP, accounting, payroll, banking, inventory, logistics, marketplace, complex e-commerce, or a Salesforce/HubSpot replacement.

## Long-Term Direction

Guided by [`product-direction.md`](./product-direction.md) (directional only — do not implement from that doc alone):

1. Strengthen existing WhatsApp CRM + campaigns + flow templates
2. Customer 360, segmentation, lightweight pipeline
3. AI customer assistant + smarter follow-ups
4. Multi-channel (Messenger, then others) on a shared conversation model
5. Appointments, catalog-for-conversations, loyalty, AI business insights

North star: successful customer relationships maintained and grown through the platform.

**Principle:** evolve the existing application — do not rebuild it.
