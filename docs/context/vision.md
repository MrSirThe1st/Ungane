# UNGANE – Vision

## Mission

Help every business in Congo and Africa turn WhatsApp conversations into customers, loyalty, and growth.

## Core Idea

WhatsApp is already the communication channel. UNGANE is the intelligence layer on top of it: CRM, booking assistant, marketing assistant, and retention engine — all through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

We are **not** selling “WhatsApp automation.”

We are selling: a digital customer assistant that helps African businesses attract, understand, and retain customers through WhatsApp.

## Why It Exists

Businesses in Congo already use WhatsApp daily, but manually:

- Customer numbers saved randomly
- No history or segmentation
- Missed follow-ups and promotions
- No structured feedback or reactivation

UNGANE makes WhatsApp a complete customer relationship system without requiring technical knowledge.

## Target Market

- Primary: Democratic Republic of Congo
- Language: French-first
- Users: small businesses — salons, spas, restaurants, shops, clinics, gyms
- Behavior: mobile-first, often on low-end devices

## Long-Term Direction

Phase 1: WhatsApp CRM + automation  
Phase 2: Advanced flow builder  
Phase 3: AI customer assistant  
Phase 4: Multi-channel (SMS, Instagram, Messenger, email)

North star: number of successful customer interactions automated through the platform.
